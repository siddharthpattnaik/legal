Privacy Policy for Traffic India: Escape the Jam (iOS)

Bundle ID: in.spattnaik.trafficindia (iPhone and iPad)
Last updated: 19 October 2026
See also: Privacy Policy for Android · Terms of Use

Traffic India: Escape the Jam ("the game") is a traffic puzzle game made by Siddharth Pattnaik ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the iPhone and iPad version does and does not do with your information. In short: the game has no account and I run no servers. Your progress is kept on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking, and sells optional items through the App Store.

iOS release status: this page describes the planned iOS version, which has not yet been released. Its advertising, purchase and tracking disclosures must be checked against the final iOS build before release. Android-only local reminders and graphics recovery are not currently implemented on iOS.

1. Information I collect

The game does not send your gameplay data to servers operated by me. The game has no sign-up, no sign-in, no in-game chat, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK of mine, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.

Third-party services process information as described in section 4. Their developer reports may include advertising, sales and diagnostic information. If you contact me for support, I receive your email address, your message and any information you choose to include, such as an order number or screenshot. I use this correspondence to answer your request and resolve problems. Please do not send passwords or full payment-card details.

2. Data stored on your device

The game keeps one small save file in its own app storage so that it can work between sessions. It is removed when you delete the game. It contains:

  • your progress: levels cleared, stars, the cities you have unlocked, your driver rank and your daily-reward streak, region and perfect-chapter rewards, daily puzzle completion, puzzle streaks and chest progress, rush records and claimed milestones;
  • your balance of coins and hints, and a ledger of coins earned and spent;
  • which in-app products you own, and the identifiers of purchases already delivered (so that nothing is granted twice);
  • your settings: music, sound, voice, vibration, graphics quality, time of day and weather, tutorial-tip history;
  • a random identifier for this installation, used only for the coin ledger. It is not an advertising identifier and is not linked to your identity.

The iOS version has no cloud save of its own. If device backups are switched on, the save file may be included in your iCloud or computer backup like any other app data; that backup is yours and is governed by Apple's terms, and I cannot read it.

3. Permissions the game requests, and why

  • Allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency) — if tracking is enabled in the released build, iOS asks whether the game may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) so that ads can be personalised and measured across apps. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the identifier is not shared and you still see ads, which are then not based on tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Network access — to load ads and to process purchases through the App Store. The game itself plays fully offline; without a connection there are simply no ads.

The game requests no access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, calendar, Bluetooth or motion data.

4. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)

The game shows two kinds of ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK: rewarded ads, which play only when you choose to watch one for a reward, and occasional full-screen ads between two levels. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google collects and processes information from your device, including IP address (from which an approximate location such as country or city can be derived), device model, operating system, language, how you interact with ads and, only if you allowed tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA). Depending on your choices and your region, Google and its advertising partners may use this to show personalised ads.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time with the AD PRIVACY button in Settings.
  • Buying No Ads or the Everything Pack removes the ads between levels. Rewarded ads remain available because they only ever play when you ask for one.

How Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Apple App Store (in-app purchases)

Optional purchases (the Everything Pack, All Cities, No Ads and coin packs) are made through Apple's In-App Purchase, using the Unity In-App Purchasing library. Payment is handled entirely by Apple: I never see your card or payment details or your Apple Account. The game receives only what it needs to deliver the item: the product identifier and the transaction identifier.

Unity

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Analytics, Unity Ads and Unity cloud crash reporting are switched off in this game.

5. Children's privacy

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact me and I will help investigate and address the request, including directing you to the relevant service.

6. Data sharing, sale and retention

I do not sell or rent your personal information. Third-party processing for ads, purchases and other services is described in section 4. The save file on your device is kept until you reset the game or delete it. Advertising data is retained by Google under its own policies, and purchase records by Apple under its own.

Support correspondence is kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and any related purchase dispute or legal obligation, then deleted when no longer needed. You can request its deletion using the contact address below; I may need to retain records where required by law.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your data: delete the game from your device. This removes the save file; there is no copy on any server of mine.
  • Tracking: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad choices: the AD PRIVACY button in the game's Settings (where shown).
  • Access, correction, deletion or objection regarding data held by Google or Apple: through your Google Account or Apple's Data and Privacy page. If you have rights under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you can also write to me at the address below and I will respond and point you to the right control. For support correspondence held by me, contact me directly.

8. Security

The save file is stored in the game's own sandboxed storage, which other apps cannot read. Data exchanged with Google's and Apple's services is sent over encrypted connections by their own software.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
